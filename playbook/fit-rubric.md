# Fit rubric (software engineering)

Score a job 0–100 as the weighted sum of five dimensions. Score every dimension against the candidate's
**career-plan.md** and **experience-library.md**; never against what a generic engineer would want.

| Dimension | Weight | 100 means | 0 means |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stack & skills** | 30 | Core languages, frameworks and infra in the JD are ones the candidate has shipped production work in (evidence in the experience library). | Primary stack is one they've never used, or the role is mostly a skill they lack (e.g. iOS for a backend engineer). |
| **Level** | 20 | JD's scope, years and title match the target level in the career plan. | Two or more levels off in either direction. |
| **Domain & problem** | 15 | Problem space matches their domain experience or a stated interest (payments, infra, AI, devtools…). | Domain they explicitly want to avoid. |
| **Company** | 15 | Stage, size, growth, engineering reputation and mission match the career plan's preferences; tier-1 target companies score high. | Signals of distress (layoffs this quarter, reposted for 6+ months), or a stage they ruled out. |
| **Logistics** | 20 | Location/remote policy, visa sponsorship, and posted comp all satisfy the career plan's constraints. | A hard constraint fails (needs sponsorship and company doesn't sponsor; onsite in a city they won't move to; comp ceiling under their floor). |

A failed **hard constraint** caps the total at 40 regardless of the other dimensions. Say which one.

## Verdict
- **< 60 → skip.** Not worth the tailoring.
- **60–74 → referral-only.** Apply only once a referral or hiring-manager conversation is in motion.
- **≥ 75 → apply.** Tailor and queue now; start the referral sequence in parallel.

## Red flags to call out
Vague JD with no team or product named; "rockstar/ninja" language; unpaid take-home over 4 hours mentioned;
comp range spanning >2× (e.g. $90k–$250k); "wear many hats" for a senior role at a 500+ person company;
recruiting agency posting with the client hidden; same role reposted repeatedly.

## Salary estimate
If the JD lacks comp, estimate a base range for this company, level and location from what you know
(levels.fyi-style bands) and label it `estimated`. Never present an estimate as posted.

## Eligibility row and evidence (shown on the desk)
Return 4–6 `eligibility` checks the way a campus placement notice lists criteria: academic cut-off if the JD names one
(candidate: B.Tech IT, CGPA 8.04), experience band vs required, the 2–3 must-have skills, location/remote, and any
career-plan dealbreaker that applies. `status: met` only with library evidence; `unmet` when clearly not; `unknown` when the
JD is silent. Then one `evidence` sentence: the JD's main ask, then the strongest true library line that answers it, in **bold**.
